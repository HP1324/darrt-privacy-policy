Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 18, 2025

This Privacy Policy describes how the mobile application Darrt (“we”, “our”, or “the app”) collects, uses, and protects your information.

1. Microphone Access (RECORD_AUDIO)

Our app requests access to your device's microphone strictly for enabling voice input. This allows you to create tasks and notes by speaking instead of typing.

We do not use this permission for any other purpose such as background listening, voice commands, or audio recording.

Audio captured via the microphone is processed locally on your device for speech-to-text conversion and is never stored or transmitted to our servers.

2. Google Drive Access

The app offers a backup and restore feature using your Google Drive. This requires signing in with your Google account to access your personal Drive storage.

We use Google Sign-In solely to obtain the secure access token required for backup and restore. We do not collect, store, or transmit any personal information such as your name, email, or profile data.

All backup operations are performed locally on your device using Google’s secure authentication. No data is sent to our servers, and your backups are stored only in your own Google Drive account.

3. Third-Party SDKs and Data Collection

Darrt uses several third-party SDKs to provide core functionality such as ads, performance monitoring, and purchase verification. These SDKs may collect certain device or usage information as described below.

a. Google Mobile Ads (AdMob) SDK

We use AdMob to display ads and gather analytics. The SDK may automatically collect and share data for advertising, analytics, and fraud prevention purposes, including:

  • IP Address: Used to estimate the general location of the device.
  • User Product Interactions: Includes app launches, taps, and video views.
  • Diagnostic Information: App crash logs, performance data, and energy usage.
  • Device and Account Identifiers: Such as Advertising ID and app set ID.

All data transmitted through the Google Mobile Ads SDK is encrypted in transit using the Transport Layer Security (TLS) protocol. Learn more about Google’s data use here: Google Privacy Policy.

b. Sentry SDK

The app uses Sentry for crash reporting and performance monitoring. Sentry helps us identify and fix bugs and improve app stability. Information collected may include:

  • Device model and operating system version
  • App version and session data
  • Battery level and network status
  • Approximate geographical region (not precise location)

This data is used only to improve performance and app reliability. It is not shared with advertisers or other third parties. Learn more at: Sentry Privacy Policy.

c. RevenueCat SDK

We use RevenueCat to manage in-app purchases and subscriptions (for example, ad removal or premium access). RevenueCat may collect:

  • Purchase history to verify premium or subscription status
  • Anonymous app user identifiers created locally by the SDK

This information is used only to confirm purchase validity and access levels and is not shared with any external third party. Learn more at: RevenueCat Privacy Policy.

d. Google Sign-In SDK

We use Google Sign-In only to authenticate your account for Google Drive backup and restore. The sign-in process is handled securely by Google. We do not store your credentials, and no data from Google Sign-In is shared with other services or stored on our servers.

4. Data Storage and Security

Darrt does not operate any external servers and does not store personal data. All sensitive operations (like backups, purchases, or sign-in) are performed securely on your device using trusted SDKs that follow industry-standard encryption.

Backup files are stored only in your personal Google Drive account.

5. Data Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or share any personal data with third parties. Any information collected by third-party SDKs (AdMob, Sentry, RevenueCat, Google) is governed by their respective privacy policies and used only for their stated purposes.

6. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy periodically. Any significant updates will be reflected on this page with an updated “Last Updated” date.
